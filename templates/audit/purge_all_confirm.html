Clear all audit logs?

This permanently and irreversibly deletes every audit log entry and login attempt record — not just entries older than the retention window, all of them, regardless of age. There is no undo, and nothing can be recovered afterward, including from a database backup taken before this point in time.

⚠ This may violate regulatory requirements

Regimes that commonly apply to a tool handling client security/health-related findings — notably HIPAA's six-year documentation retention requirement — expect audit trails to be kept for a fixed minimum period, not cleared on demand. This action exists purely as a storage-management escape hatch. Confirm your organization's own retention obligations before using it; RedScribe cannot determine what applies to your deployment.

Finding structure

These are the narrative content sections a Finding and a Catalogue entry can carry — add, relabel, reorder, or retire them freely. Retiring a section (uncheck "Active") hides it from create/edit/detail pages and blocks new comments on it, but never deletes content already saved against it — re-activate it any time to see that content again. Deleting a section below is permanent and takes its stored content with it.

This defines the structure of every finding, past and present

Every finding and catalogue entry — on active, archived, and already-delivered engagements — reads its content through the sections defined here. Relabeling, retiring, reordering, or deleting a section changes how that finding's existing content is shown (or hides it) everywhere, immediately, including reports already generated from live data. Changes here are not scoped to new engagements only.
